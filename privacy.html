Information we collect

When you submit an appointment request, we collect the details you enter: your name, mobile number, email address (optional), city, preferred consultation location, main concern, whether you have an MRI report, how long you have had symptoms, and any additional information you choose to provide.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your enquiry and arrange a consultation.
  • To prepare for your appointment.

Submitting the form does not establish a doctor–patient relationship or provide a medical diagnosis. Please do not send MRI images or detailed medical records through the website.

Sharing

Your details are used only by the appointment team for the purposes above and are not sold. Details of any service providers used to process requests will be added here.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information you submitted. Contact details for privacy requests are to be confirmed.
